## About CRC The Peridona

<a id="about"></a>

CRC The Peridona is an ultra-luxury residential development on 6.80 acres inside the 452-acre Jaypee Greens golf township at C1, Jaypee Greens, Greater Noida, developed by <a href="https://www.crcperidona.in" className="text-blue-600 underline">CRC Group</a> — the Delhi NCR developer led by Founder &amp; CEO Kunal Bhalla under the positioning &ldquo;Building Values&rdquo;. The name comes from the Greek &ldquo;Peridona&rdquo; — &ldquo;giving plenty&rdquo;, an emblem of abundance, prosperity and good fortune.

The project comprises 7 towers of 43 floors each, rising to approximately 160 m* with a 330 m* long front facade, and houses only 341 residences. It follows a One Floor One Apartment* concept with private lift lobbies — so every residence enjoys two-side views: the golf course to the north and the 60-acre nature park to the south. To the east lie the practice range and Jaypee residences; to the west, the township clubhouse and waterbodies.

## Residences & Configurations

<a id="residences"></a>

The Peridona offers 4 BHK, 4.5 BHK and 5.5 BHK residences starting from 4,950 sq ft, each occupying its own floor plate with a private lift lobby.

### 4 BHK Residences

Expansive 4 BHK layouts beginning at 4,900 sq ft, with imported marble in living and dining areas, engineered wood in bedrooms, and double-glazed thermally broken aluminium windows framing golf course and nature park views on both sides.

### 5 BHK Residences

At 5,600 sq ft, the 5 BHK residences add generous family and entertainment spaces — modular kitchens with premium fittings and built-in appliances, European-brand sanitaryware and CP fittings, and VRV/VRF air conditioning throughout.

### 4 BHK Grand Residences

A larger 4 BHK format at 6,700 sq ft for buyers who want fewer bedrooms and dramatically more living space — grand receptions, private studies and wide golf-facing decks.

### Penthouses

Crowning the towers are penthouses (indicative, subject to approved plan) — the most exclusive homes at The Peridona, positioned above the city with uninterrupted township views.

All residences include smart-home provisions for lighting, climate and security, video door phones, fire safety systems, 24x7 multi-tier security, and high-speed lifts with private lobby access.
