## Understanding the Airport Effect on Real Estate
<a id="airport-effect"></a>


Before we look at what makes **CRC The Peridona** distinct, it is worth understanding why airports have such a powerful and consistent effect on nearby property markets — globally and in India.

### The Global Pattern
Major new airports have historically anchored property appreciation corridors around them. The examples are well-documented:

- **Dubai International Airport** turned the surrounding desert into one of the world&apos;s most valuable real estate markets  
- **Amsterdam Schiphol** anchored an entire economic ecosystem of hotels, logistics hubs, and premium residential communities  
- **Chicago O&apos;Hare** supported decades of sustained growth across neighbouring suburbs  

Airports don&apos;t just move passengers — they move **money, employment, and ambition** into a region. Every new airport creates demand for hotels, logistics, corporate offices, and ultimately, premium homes for the people who run all of it.

### The Gurgaon Precedent — India's Closest Parallel
The closest Indian parallel is **Gurgaon&apos;s transformation alongside the IGI Airport corridor in the 2000s** — from farmland and scattered industrial plots into NCR&apos;s most expensive residential and commercial market.

Greater Noida in 2026 carries similar structural ingredients:
- Proximity to a major new international airport  
- Planned city infrastructure with wide expressway connectivity  
- A significantly earlier stage of its luxury pricing cycle than Gurgaon  
- A wave of corporate and aviation-linked interest in the corridor  

The difference this time: in Gurgaon, ultra-luxury supply eventually flooded in. At Jaypee Greens, the township is essentially complete — only around 3,600 units across 452 acres — which makes new ultra-luxury supply inside it structurally scarce.
