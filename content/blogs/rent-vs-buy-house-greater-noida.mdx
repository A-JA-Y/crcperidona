This page works it through for a 4 BHK at CRC The Peridona in Jaypee Greens, from ₹12.5 crore\*. It is arithmetic, not advice.

## Rent and Price in Jaypee Greens

**Rent.** A 4 BHK for rent in Jaypee Greens of about 4,600 sq. ft. asks around ₹2 lakh a month. Smaller 4 BHKs of about 3,100 sq. ft. start near ₹86,000. This page uses ₹2 lakh for a home of similar size.

**Price.** The 4 BHK here starts at 4,950 sq. ft. and a tentative ₹12.5 crore\*, about ₹25,000 per sq. ft. The Jaypee Greens average is about ₹14,450 per sq. ft., so this is priced well above the neighbourhood.

**Yield.** A year’s rent of ₹24 lakh is about 1.9% of the price. Put another way, the price equals 52 years of today’s rent.

## Rent vs EMI: A Worked Example

**Buying**

- Price: ₹12.5 crore
- Down payment at 25%: ₹3.125 crore. Banks lend at most 75% on loans above ₹75 lakh.
- GST at 5%: ₹62.5 lakh
- Stamp duty in Greater Noida at 7% plus 1% registration: ₹1 crore
- Your own money in total: about ₹4.75 crore
- Loan: ₹9.375 crore
- EMI at 8% for 20 years: about ₹7.84 lakh a month
- Upkeep a tenant wouldn’t pay: say ₹40,000 a month

**Renting**

- A similar home: about ₹2 lakh a month, rising about 5% a year

## Tax on Rent and Home Loans

- **Tenants:** HRA relief is available only in the old regime, and the salary-based limit in Greater Noida is 40%.
- **TDS on rent:** an individual paying more than ₹50,000 a month must deduct 2%. On ₹2 lakh a month that is ₹48,000 a year.
- **Owners, old regime:** up to ₹2 lakh of interest a year on a self-occupied home. Against ₹75 lakh of yearly interest, it hardly counts.
- **New regime:** neither HRA nor the interest deduction.

Check your own position with a tax adviser.

## Conclusion

On rent vs buy house in Greater Noida, the top end doesn’t work on rent arithmetic. Owning a ₹12.5 crore home costs about ₹85 lakh to ₹95 lakh a year more than renting one like it, and the home has to gain about 8% a year to make that up. If this is the home you want for the next twenty years and the outlay doesn’t strain you, that is a fair reason to buy. If you’re buying mainly for returns, compare it honestly with what the same money would earn elsewhere.
