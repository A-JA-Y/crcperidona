  In a move that has drawn close attention across Greater Noida&apos;s luxury property market, the Greater Noida Authority approved a 3.58% revision in circle rates — the first such revision in nearly nine years. For homebuyers and investors evaluating CRC The Peridona at Jaypee Greens, Greater Noida, this is a signal that demands close attention.

  The revision is being viewed as a long-overdue correction that narrows the gap between government-notified rates and the market prices seen across Greater Noida&apos;s premium residential zones. CRC The Peridona — 341 ultra-luxury residences rising inside the 452-acre Jaypee Greens golf township, home to a 130-acre, 18-hole Greg Norman Design Championship Course — sits squarely at the intersection of this policy shift.

  ## Why this revision matters right now
  <a id="importance"></a>

  Circle rates — the minimum benchmark valuations set by the government for property registration — had remained static for years even as market activity in Greater Noida&apos;s premium corridors strengthened. A widening gap had emerged between official rates and real transaction values in the area&apos;s most sought-after addresses. The revision signals the government&apos;s intent to begin catching up — and signals institutional confidence in the market.

  For buyers planning to download the CRC The Peridona brochure and move toward a purchase, the immediate practical impact is a modest uptick in stamp duty and registration charges — calculated on the higher of the circle rate or the actual transaction price. Since ultra-luxury residences at Jaypee Greens transact well above circle rates, the direct cost impact on Peridona buyers is limited. But the signal it sends is powerful: official valuations in this corridor are moving up, not down.

  ## CRC The Peridona: the project at a glance
  <a id="project-glance"></a>

  Developed by CRC Group — the Delhi NCR developer led by Founder &amp; CEO Kunal Bhalla under the positioning &ldquo;Building Values&rdquo; — CRC The Peridona is an ultra-luxury development of just 341 residences across 7 towers of 43 floors, on 6.80 acres inside the 452-acre Jaypee Greens township. The project follows a One Floor One Apartment* concept with private lift lobbies, giving every residence two-side views of the golf course and the 60-acre nature park. Typologies span 4 BHK, 4.5 BHK and 5.5 BHK residences starting from 4,950 sq ft.

  ## Infrastructure context amplifies the circle rate signal
  <a id="infrastructure"></a>

  The rate revision lands in a corridor already rich with infrastructure momentum. Jaypee Greens sits ~2 km from Pari Chowk with direct access to the Yamuna Expressway and connectivity to the Noida–Greater Noida Expressway. The Noida International Airport at Jewar — approximately 35 km away via the Yamuna Expressway — anchors the region&apos;s long-term growth story, while Delhi&apos;s IGI Airport remains about 55 km away and the Pari Chowk / Knowledge Park metro is close by.

  For the luxury segment specifically, the combination is potent: a mature, low-density township of only ~3,600 units across 452 acres, a hard-capped new supply of 341 Peridona residences, and a policy environment in which official valuations are being marked upward.
